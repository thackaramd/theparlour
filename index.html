Until next time

Issue One of The Parlour is now out in the world. Thank you to everyone who helped bring it there — to those who sent work, trusted the magazine with their writing and art, read its pages, came along to our first gathering, bought a copy, or simply took an interest in this small beginning.

What began as an idea for a place in which literature might bring people together became, for a little while, exactly that. I’m very grateful to everyone who entered into it.

For now, the parlour is quiet. We’ll open the door again in the new year.

Until then, thank you for being part of Issue One.

Max Thackara
Editor, The Parlour
