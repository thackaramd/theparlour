THE PARLOUR

A new literary magazine for all voices.

Issue One Publication

Thank you to everyone who has supported The Parlour over these past few months.

I'm pleased to say that the inaugural issue is now complete and ready to be shared.

To coincide with its publication, there will be a small, informal gathering at Maison Le Vôtre in Bedford from 2pm on Sunday 23rd August.

Rather than a formal launch, the afternoon is simply an opportunity to spend some time with contributors, readers, and others who have been part of the magazine's beginning. Contributor copies will be available to collect, and everyone who submitted work for the inaugural issue is welcome to pick up a complimentary copy. For those contributors who are unable to attend, I will be in touch separately to arrange delivery of your copy.

Copies of the magazine will also be available to purchase if you're discovering The Parlour for the first time.

Whether you've written for the magazine, submitted work, supported it from the beginning, or are simply curious to see what has taken shape, you'd be very welcome.

There is no formal programme, and you're very welcome to stay for as much or as little of the afternoon as you like.

For any questions in the meantime, you're welcome to write to editor@theparlour.org.uk.

Max Thackara
Editor, The Parlour
